During GCMC simulations, it is essential to identify the adsorption sites on the catalyst surface. In this work, the adsorption sites on both the surface and in the bulk phase were accurately determined using the PH-SA method（Zheng, S. et al. Active phase discovery in heterogeneous catalysis via topology-guided sampling and machine learning. Zenodo, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14777320 (2025).） developed by our research group, with the output provided in the adsorption_sites.json file.
The adsorption_sites.json file contains the surface adsorption site information extracted from the POSCAR file.
